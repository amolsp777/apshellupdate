A Bitcoin Core wallet rebroadcast side channel lets an adversary bind an on-chain address to the IP of the node whose wallet tracks it. Developer ismaelsadeeq disclosed the technique on Delving Bitcoin on November 25, 2026. In a regtest simulation, attribution succeeded in 30 of 30 attempts. Bitcoin Core wallets re-announce unconfirmed transactions to peers
