Hackers are using fake AI websites to push Net Guard, a Microsoft Store-signed application that researchers classified as browser-hijacking malware. The attack starts with typing mistakes in popular AI service addresses and redirects visitors to misleading security checks. Traced 186 lookalike domains covering 27 AI brands, including Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSee
