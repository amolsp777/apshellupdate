Hackers compromised three Linux GPU hosts in a cryptomining campaign that combined hidden mining processes, fake GPU readings, and multiple remote access channels. Tracked as REF3364, the intrusion used SANSAR, a Rust program that controls CPU and GPU miners through Tor. Although SANSAR manages the operation, a separate kernel rootkit hides its activity. Ano
