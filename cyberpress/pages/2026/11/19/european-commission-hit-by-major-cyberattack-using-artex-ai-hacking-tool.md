The European Commission took several applications offline after CERT-EU detected a cyber campaign involving ARTEX, an AI-powered penetration-testing framework, POLITICO reported on November 19, 2026. The disruption lasted more than 10 days, affecting systems that staff use to access payslips, submit timesheets, request leave and claim medical reimbursements.
