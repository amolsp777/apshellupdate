A hacker known as BabidiGhost used a jailbroken AI agent to steal an insurer’s customer database and about 8.6 GB of files from municipalities and other public bodies. The nine-day campaign targeted more than 50 organizations and datasets, mainly in Belgium, France and Switzerland. Researchers found no evidence that the operator used a zero-day vulnerability
