WordPress.org blocked malicious updates for six plugins developed by WP connect on November 22, 2026, before the releases reached users. The plugins had more than 3,600 active installations combined. That figure measures the reach of the targeted plugins, not the number of compromised sites. WordPress.org closed all six plugins in its directory that evening.
